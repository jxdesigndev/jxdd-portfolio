The Present

Igbo Heritage & Code

The neon green and the scattered marks across this site are not trend — they're intentional. I built them without copying any existing script, but they carry the same instinct my Igbo ancestors had for turning meaning into symbol. Most of what “design” means online today traces back to Europe, Japan, the U.S. I wanted something that traced back to me. This is what that looks like.

The Method

Systems Over Aesthetics

Every project starts with a single question: what decision does this design make easier? Not “does it look good?” — but “does it work?” Beauty that doesn't reduce friction is decoration. Systems that don't get used are theory. I build at the intersection: beautiful systems that humans actually need.
