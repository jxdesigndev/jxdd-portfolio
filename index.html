About Me

A Builder Who
Designs, Codes,
Secures ✦ Automates

I'm Okezie Ferdinand — a system designer and builder from Nigeria. I started as a digital artist in 2020, transforming photos into cartoon art. In 2024 I rebranded JX Design & Dev with a new mission: build systems that make life easier, safer, and better.

I operate across four disciplines — UI/UX design for security and health systems, vibe coding with Lovable, Bolt and Framer, cybersecurity (actively learning), and automation using n8n. Building toward a future where design, code, security and automation are one.
